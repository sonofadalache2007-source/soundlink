SoundLink Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 16, 2026

SoundLink ("we", "our", or "us") provides a collaborative audio engineering, escrow management, and stem workspace application. This Privacy Policy outlines how your personal data, payment information, and audio deliverables are collected, stored, and protected.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Details: Basic profile credentials, display name, and communication preferences.
  • Audio Stems & Deliverables: WAV/MP3 files, project notes, plugin parameters, and revision markers uploaded to your shared workspace.
  • Payment & Transaction Data: Transaction tokens, escrow milestone state, and subscription receipts processed securely via RevenueCat and payment partners (e.g. Stripe, Google Play). SoundLink does not store complete credit card numbers on our servers.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To facilitate milestone tracking and A/B deliverable reviews between artists, vocalists, and audio engineers.
  • To process escrow payments, subscription passes, and producer payouts through RevenueCat Web Billing.
  • To deliver notifications regarding stem updates, revisions, and milestone sign-offs.

3. Third-Party Payment Processing (RevenueCat)

We utilize RevenueCat to manage in-app subscriptions, digital product entitlements, and escrow transaction billing. When you subscribe or make an escrow deposit, your transaction is processed in accordance with RevenueCat's Privacy Policy and relevant app store platforms.

4. Audio Ownership & Intellectual Property

SoundLink does not claim ownership of user-uploaded vocal stems, compositions, or master recordings. All rights to project deliverables transfer directly to the client upon escrow approval and release.

5. Data Security

We implement industry-standard 256-bit encryption for all data in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and secure access controls to ensure your private audio stems remain confidential.
